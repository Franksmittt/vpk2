================================================================================
VAALPENSKRAAL GAME RESERVE: PROJECT, DESIGN, PAGES, COMPONENTS & SPECIES
================================================================================
Single reference for sales, agents, developers, and accessibility. Written so a
reader can form a clear mental picture: stack, visual language, every route,
SEO and structured data, motion and accessibility choices, every reusable UI
building block, and every quarry row aligned with data/species.ts.

Update when routes, components, or QUARRY_SPECIES change. Verify permits,
quotas, and legal minima with management before external use.

For user-visible facts about services, species, camp, compliance, and
geography, treat client-business-truth.json at the repo root as the alignment
source alongside this file.

Last aligned with codebase: 16 April 2026 (editorial PageHero on all marketing
routes matching the /species hero scale; sticky quarry navigation with
full-bleed strip photos and a uniform 20% black wash; /species hub with
Suspense, URL-driven compare, grouped roster and jump nav; /the-hunt,

HOW TO READ THIS DOCUMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Sections run top to bottom: positioning, hunting/lodge features, copy voice,
  SEO and discovery, then the full technical picture (stack, colors, fonts,
  layout, motion, accessibility), then every page URL, then every component file
  under components/, then data and lib files, then the complete species table.
• "Site order" in data/species.ts is the array order of QUARRY_SPECIES (stable
  ids and card ordering tools). On /species the visible directory is grouped by
  SPECIES_GROUP_ORDER in data/species.ts (woodland, plains, bush-water,
  herd-morphs, dangerous last).
• Quarry count in product data and JSON is 22 species. Do not reintroduce
  removed quarry (for example Common Eland) unless management restores it in
  data and client-business-truth.json together.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LOCATION & POSITIONING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Waterberg Biosphere, Limpopo, South Africa: bushveld hunting and lodge stay.
• Suited to international and South African hunters; estate copy emphasises
  fair chase, ethical harvest, and honest fieldcraft in thick bush and open
  moments ("Earnest Ally" tone: casual, serious, respectful, short sentences,
  sensory language, no price justification in prose).
• Exact gate coordinates and directions are supplied when a booking is
  confirmed (see client-business-truth.json geography.gateDirectionsPolicy).
• Firearm temporary import, trophy export, and home-country import rules remain
  the client's responsibility with their own agents; the estate does not
  process client paperwork on their behalf.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HUNTING & ESTATE: FEATURES & BENEFITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Plains game and dangerous game (where applicable) under professional hunting
  oversight; species aligned with census, ethics, and carrying capacity.
• Waterberg thicket and mixed terrain: technical stalking, wind discipline,
  and real bush conditions rather than a "brochure circuit" experience.
• Optional clay pigeon shooting and game drives for rest days or non-hunters.
  Scheduling follows camp workload; confirm when you book.
• Professional meat handling narrative on site: slaughtering, salting of hides,
  carcass weighing; cold room for proper meat chain after the shot.
• On-site safe facilities for valuables (documents, cash, small electronics).
• P3 Exemption Permit called out in marketing: confirm current certificates
  and scope with management when booking.
• Taxidermy: outsourced to professionals the client appoints; international
  shipping and export documentation sit with the client and their taxidermist.
  The estate does not act as agent for that paperwork.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LODGING & CAMP: FEATURES & BENEFITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OVERNIGHT MODEL
• Four guest chalets only: no separate main residence for hunters on camp;
  communal areas are for day and evening use. Up to ~20 guests camp-wide when
  chalets are full (4–5 guests per chalet).

NAMED CHALETS (identical layout inside)
• Roan Kraal
• Eland Kraal
• Gemsbok Kraal
• Koedoe Kraal

Each chalet: four single beds + one double, air conditioning, full bathroom
(showers, two basins, toilet).

COMMUNAL HUB
• Bar name on camp: Vark Kraal (communal bar, kitchen, and lounge / dining).
• Gas stove and electric oven for real catering workflow.
• Fully catered: snacks, lunch, supper; food, soft drinks, and alcohol included
  under responsible service guidelines (not metered like a hotel bar).

OUTDOOR & VIEWING
• Swimming pool.
• Boma under trees with canopy: evening fire and social space.
• Outlook point: elevated game viewing and sundowner-friendly.
• Waterhole: passive game viewing; low impact, ethical viewing rhythm.

LOGISTICS & SECURITY
• Gated access; Matlabas Protection Service referenced in site copy.
• Optional shuttle: enquire when booking (for example Johannesburg or Pretoria
  timing; verify with management).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GUEST OUTCOMES (BENEFITS SUMMARY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• One base camp: sleep in named kraals, share Vark Kraal and facilities: clear
  expectations for groups, families, and international parties.
• Full board reduces friction: focus on the hunt and the bush, not invoices
  at every meal.
• Infrastructure for serious hunters: cold chain, safes, meat and hide services,
  outlook / waterhole downtime without leaving the property narrative.
• Transparent stance on paperwork: clients use their own agents for rifles
  and trophies across borders.

PART A: TECH STACK & PROJECT SHAPE
================================================================================
FRAMEWORK & TOOLING
• Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript.
• Tailwind CSS v4 with @import "tailwindcss" and @theme tokens in app/globals.css.

  mailto:info@vaalpenskraal.com with a prefilled body.

  (align visible copy with client-business-truth.json).

LAYOUT UTILITIES (globals.css, @layer utilities)
• editorial-container: max-w-7xl, horizontal padding (px-4 sm:px-6 md:px-12).

• eyebrow: small caps label style (uppercase, wide tracking, muted).

  classes; not every page uses all of them.

MOTION, SCROLL, AND FINAL-PHASE POLISH
• Home hero: components/home/HeroCanvasScrollSection.tsx (client). One viewport
  tall (min 100svh / 100dvh), no scroll runway: wheel and trackpad move the page
  normally. Three full-bleed Next/Image stills crossfade on a timer with dot
  controls (default set: /images/hero/hero-legacy.png,
  /images/hero/hero-encounter.png, /images/hero/estate-bushveld-wide.png).
  Autoplay pauses on hover or focus; prefers-reduced-motion disables the timer.
  public/images/hero-sequence/ remains optional for future frame-sequence work;
  see public/images/hero-sequence/README.txt (the live home hero does not read
  those frames today).
• Home body: components/home/HomePageClient.tsx wraps the hero in
  HeroCanvasScrollSection; bento and downstream sections use Motion and fluid
  min-heights where updated (avoid rigid px min-heights on text-heavy cards).
• Scroll-linked CSS typography (app/globals.css): classes vp-scroll-hero-*,
  vp-scroll-root-line, vp-scroll-reveal-type. Uses @keyframes vp-typo-rise /
  vp-typo-fade with rotateX and translateZ in transform space. Under
  prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference and @supports:
  - Page hero lines use animation-timeline: scroll(root block) with tuned
    animation-range (native scroll-driven animation).
  - In-section headings use animation-timeline: view(block) for entry-linked
    motion.
  - @starting-style provides initial opacity where supported; fallbacks set
    opacity: 1 when scroll() or view() timelines are not supported.
• PageHero (components/layout/PageHero.tsx): shared marketing hero aligned with
  the /species main hero band: min-h-[min(100svh,920px)], full-bleed Image,
  two-layer scrim (bg-black/18 plus gradient-to-t from-black/58 via-black/26),
  pill eyebrow with Mountain icon, title clamp text-[clamp(2.25rem,6vw,4rem)],
  leading and tracking matched to species, max-w-3xl text stack, pb/pt bands
  pb-16 pt-28 md:pb-24 md:pt-32. Optional scrollLinkedTypography wraps copy in
  vp-scroll-hero-3d with perspective and applies vp-scroll-hero-eyebrow to the
  pill when enabled. Used on /experience, /lodge, /reserve, /the-hunt,
  /activities, /gallery (not on /contact). Background: imageSrc when set, else
  picsum seed.

ACCESSIBILITY AND ZOOM (WCAG 1.4.4 orientation)
• Prefer fluid min-heights (min(), rem, dvh) over fixed px stacks on species
  monograph media shells and similar placeholders so large text / 200% zoom
  clips less aggressively (for example MonographMedia.tsx, GreaterKuduGuide.tsx,
  SpeciesMonographGuide.tsx).
• Editorial grids rely on wrapping columns; avoid fixed pixel heights on pure
  text blocks.

IMAGE STRATEGY (brief)
• Local files under public/images/ (lodge, home, hero, optional hero-sequence,
  gallery, outlook, activities, species/cards, species monograph folders,
  experience photography, the-hunt). URLs in code are root-absolute paths such
  as /images/hero/estate-bushveld-wide.png (Next.js static file convention).
• species-media.ts maps each species id to /images/species/cards/{id}.png.
• Species index hero uses SPECIES_PAGE_HERO_IMAGE (iron mountain quarry scene).
• Greater kudu deep guide still uses greater-kudu folder assets where coded.
• Experience page senses tiles use the experience-senses-* filenames under
  public/images/experience/ (wired in app/experience/page.tsx).
• Discarded or duplicate marketing stills that should not ship live sit under
  unused-images/ at the repo root, not under public/.

STICKY QUARRY NAVIGATION (breadcrumb strip)
• Sticky jump bars use a solid dark bar (bg-black/90, light backdrop-blur-md,
  border-b) only. No second hero image behind the strip (avoids duplicate crop
  with the page hero).
• Order: full-width hero first, then sticky TOC or back link (SpeciesPageContent,
  SpeciesMonographGuide, GreaterKuduGuide, SpeciesStubDetail).
