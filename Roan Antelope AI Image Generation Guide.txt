Reference Guide for Latent Space Generation: Roan Antelope
1) Taxonomy and scope
The roan antelope, strictly designated by the binomial nomenclature Hippotragus equinus, is a massive, highly adaptable African bovid belonging to the subfamily Hippotraginae.1 Within the context of generative artificial intelligence and latent space rendering, prompt engineers must permanently anchor this target subject using the full binomial alongside the "African bovid" token. This foundational anchoring prevents the model from hallucinating equine anatomy, a common failure mode triggered by the specific epithet equinus, which historically references the species' horse-like build and erect mane.1
In South Africa, the regional phenotypes most relevant to private estates and hunting reserves are typically derived from the southern subspecies, Hippotragus equinus equinus, as well as the central African Hippotragus equinus cottoni.2 Historically, the distribution of this species spanned vast tracts of sub-Saharan Africa, but human encroachment has severely fractured its range.4 While populations in national sanctuaries such as the Kruger National Park have dwindled to critically low numbers (approximately 70 individuals), the private game ranching industry in South Africa now manages over 3500 individuals, making these specific southern and central phenotypes the most commercially relevant targets for visual asset generation.5 Furthermore, extensive genetic admixture has occurred on private farms due to the historical importation of the West African subspecies (Hippotragus equinus koba), meaning prompt engineers will frequently encounter blended phenotypic traits in contemporary reference photographs.5
The most severe structural and chromatic challenge in generating the roan antelope is explicitly separating it from its sister species, the sable antelope (Hippotragus niger), as well as the extinct bluebuck (Hippotragus leucophaeus).1 Generative diffusion models suffer from extreme conceptual bleed between the roan and the sable. The roan is strictly defined by its namesake "strawberry roan" coat, a grizzled blend of greyish-red, amber, and brown guard hairs that transitions sharply into a stark white underbelly.1 The face features a bold black and white geometric mask that is lighter in females.1
Sidebar: Estate Colour Morphs (Palette Only) Within the high-value South African game ranching sector, selective breeding has resulted in the "golden roan" colour morph.1 If an industry client requires this specific phenotype, prompt engineers must manipulate the prompt palette exclusively without altering the underlying anatomical mesh. The golden roan expresses a highly diluted, pale golden-fawn to erythristic pelage. To achieve this without triggering algorithmic failure modes, engineers should utilize specific hex codes (such as #A67B5B) and descriptive terms like "pale dilute fawn" or "erythristic African bovid".9 It is absolutely critical to apply heavy negative weights to terms like "golden retriever", "metallic", or "shiny" to prevent the model from rendering domestic dog anatomy or applying an unnatural, reflective metallic sheen to the animal's coat.
2) Confusion pairs

Because these animals share overlapping taxonomic tags, the latent space will frequently merge anatomical traits from more commonly photographed species into the Hippotragus equinus output. To enforce anatomical isolation, prompt engineers must utilize aggressive negative prompting based on the strict boundaries outlined in the morphological comparison matrix below. The sable antelope (Hippotragus niger) represents the highest risk of conceptual bleed, followed closely by the gemsbok (Oryx gazella) and the greater kudu (Tragelaphus strepsiceros).

3) Sexual dimorphism
Achieving reference-grade authenticity requires a rigorous understanding of the sexual dimorphism present in the roan antelope. Unlike species where females are entirely hornless or display a radically different base coat colour, the dimorphism in Hippotragus equinus is subtle, structural, and heavily dependent on specific rendering of mass and facial contrast.
Both male and female roan antelopes possess heavily ridged, backward-curving scimitar horns.2 A common algorithmic failure involves the generation of hornless females, driven by the AI's underlying training data on cervids and smaller bovids. Prompt engineers must explicitly dictate horn generation for both sexes. The dimorphism dictates that male horns are substantially thicker at the base and longer, frequently reaching between 60 and 100 cm in a sweeping arc designed for clashing during intrasexual combat.15 Female horns are visibly more slender, slightly shorter, and function mechanically as stabbing weapons for defence.17
The scale of the animal provides the next critical dimorphic cue. The roan is among the largest of the African antelopes, surpassed only by the eland and the African buffalo.4 Males achieve an imposing mass, weighing between 260 and 300 kg, and stand up to 160 cm at the shoulder.2 Females are slightly lighter, ranging from 225 to 275 kg.2 Visually, this mass difference must be concentrated in the neck and shoulder cues. Bulls possess a dramatically thickened, muscular neck required to support their cranial weaponry, while cows exhibit a more refined, narrower cervical structure.
The facial mask undergoes distinct changes based on age and sex. In dominant mature males, the geometric mask covering the bridge of the snout and surrounding the eyes is a stark, high-contrast jet black.2 In adult females, this same geometric pattern is typically rendered in a softer charcoal or dark reddish-brown.2 Neonates and young calves lack this high-contrast mask almost entirely, presenting a lighter, uniform reddish-brown face that aids in camouflage during their critical hiding phase.2
Finally, the preorbital glands serve as a major failure point in AI rendering. Located anterior to the medial canthus of each eye, these exocrine scent glands appear as trenchlike slits of dark blue to black, nearly bare skin.18 They contain sebaceous and sudoriferous glands used for territorial marking.18 On camera, these glands read as a subtle, dry anatomical crease. Diffusion models frequently misinterpret this structure, generating horrific anomalies such as weeping tear ducts, open bloody wounds, or secondary pupils. Prompt logic must strictly define this as a "subtle dark skin fold" while heavily penalizing anatomical body horror tokens.
4) Morphology shot guide C1 through C13
This section outlines the precise compositional requirements for generating the 13 foundational reference images required for the estate monograph. Each shot demands absolute adherence to South African environmental context, morphological accuracy, and the mitigation of specific AI failure modes.
C1: full body
The full body composition must serve as the primary anatomical reference for the roan antelope. The camera should be positioned perfectly broadside, capturing the animal at mid-flank height to eliminate any perspective distortion of the limbs or backline. The lighting must evoke the harsh, directional sunlight typical of the South African bushveld during late afternoon, which will cast deep, defining shadows beneath the heavy, muscular neck and highlight the coarse texture of the strawberry roan pelage.16 The background requires extreme depth of field management, reducing the typical savanna flora into a completely defocused mass of green and brown bokeh, ensuring no specific geographical landmarks are identifiable.19
Anatomically, the model must respect the distinct sloping backline of the roan, which peaks sharply at the withers and descends gradually toward the pelvic girdle.16 The ears are a defining feature and must be rendered exceptionally long and narrow, possessing a distinctly donkey-like appearance, and terminating in highly visible dark brown hair tufts.20 The long legs must transition from the lighter body tone into distinct, dark brown to black "socks" beginning at the carpal and tarsal joints.20 The short, erect, mohawk-like mane must run clearly from the back of the neck to the withers.1
* Must show: Full profile from nose to tufted tail, sloping backline, exact four-leg placement, dark leg socks, erect mane.
* Must avoid: Level backlines resembling a horse, missing ear tufts, obstruction of the hooves by foreground grass.
* AI failure modes: Generative models frequently hallucinate limb counts in ungulates. The prompt must explicitly enforce bilateral consistency. You must aggressively negative prompt against "three legs, five legs, fused limbs, conjoined anatomy, floating hooves".
C2: eyes
This image requires a dedicated macro or telephoto focal length, cropping tightly onto the orbital region of the skull. The lighting should catch a sharp, specular corneal highlight that reflects the vast, open horizon of the African savanna, adding a layer of hyper-realism to the render. The iris itself must be a rich, deep, and completely uniform brown, which is characteristic of the vast majority of African bovids.21
The absolute defining structural rule for this shot is the lateral placement of the eye on the skull and the strictly horizontal orientation of the pupil.21 This evolutionary adaptation grants the antelope a panoramic visual field covering 320 to 340 degrees, allowing it to detect predators while grazing.21 The skin immediately surrounding the orbit must transition seamlessly from short, coarse roan hairs into the stark white patch that forms the upper portion of the facial mask. Eyelashes should be thick, dark, and protective against the dusty environment.
* Must show: Deep brown iris, distinctly horizontal pupil, lateral skull placement, crisp dark eyelashes.
* Must avoid: Forward-facing binocular eye placement, any visible white sclera.
* AI failure modes: Latent space generation holds a massive bias toward human, canine, and feline ocular anatomy. The model will consistently attempt to render round or vertical-slit pupils. Negative prompt aggressively against "round pupil, human eye, vertical slit pupil, white sclera, blue iris, heterochromia, forward facing eyes".
C3: horns (both sexes as applicable)
This composition dictates a dramatic low-angle perspective, positioning the camera below the head and looking upward at the cranial weaponry against a blown-out, high-key sky or a softly out-of-focus canopy of Vachellia branches. The focal plane must lock entirely onto the keratinous structure of the horns. The horns must originate closely together on the frontal bone of the skull and sweep backward in a smooth, continuous, and symmetrical scimitar arc.2
The textural rendering of the ridging, or annulations, is the most critical aspect of this shot. These ridges must be deep, heavily pronounced, and perfectly parallel, running from the thick base of the horn up through the lower three-quarters of the shaft.2 The final upper quarter of the horn must transition smoothly into a sharp, unridged, darker tip.2 If rendering a mature bull, the bases must be overwhelmingly thick and robust.
* Must show: Backward-sweeping scimitar arc, deep parallel ridges terminating before a smooth tip, thick bases.
* Must avoid: Forward hooks, lateral spiraling, perfectly smooth bases, ivory-coloured tips.
* AI failure modes: The AI will reflexively attempt to merge the Hippotragus horn geometry with the corkscrew spirals of the greater kudu (Tragelaphus) or the straight spears of the gemsbok (Oryx). You must use strict negative prompts for "kudu corkscrew, gemsbok spear, wildebeest hooks, spiraled horns, straight horns, flat horns".
C4: female
The female portrait must capture the subtle, refined dimorphism of the cow while maintaining her status as a massive, powerful animal. The lighting should be soft and diffuse, perhaps simulating morning light filtering through Colophospermum mopane leaves, which highlights the dusty, grizzled strawberry roan texture of her coat.23 The camera angle should be a three-quarter frontal profile to properly display both the facial mask and the sweep of the horns.
The critical token adjustment here lies in the facial mask. Instead of jet black, the female's mask must be rendered in deep charcoal, dark brown, or rufous, blending slightly more gently into the stark white patches around the eyes and muzzle.2 Her horns must be fully present and curving backward, but they must be visibly more slender and slightly less aggressively ridged than those of a mature male.2 If the lower abdomen is visible within the frame, the model must correctly render an udder possessing exactly four teats.2
* Must show: Dark brown or charcoal face mask, slender but ridged backward-curving horns, proportional neck, four teats.
* Must avoid: Jet black face masks, exaggerated bulging neck musculature, lack of horns.
* AI failure modes: Algorithmic bias heavily favours hornless females due to the prevalence of deer and certain gazelles in the training data. Furthermore, models will frequently hallucinate male genitalia on female frames. Explicitly negative prompt against "hornless, missing horns, male genitalia, thick horns, jet black mask".
C5: male
This composition is designed to project dominance, mass, and territorial presence. A tight, head-and-shoulders crop taken from a slightly lower elevation will emphasize the bulk of the bull. The lighting should utilize harsh, direct midday sun to cast deep, aggressive shadows across the facial mask, maximizing the stark, high-contrast division between the jet-black bridge of the nose and the pure white cheek patches.2
The neck must be rendered as incredibly thick, bulging with the heavy musculature required to wield the massive, heavily ridged horns that dominate the upper boundary of the frame.16 The short, stiff, mohawk-like mane must stand rigidly erect along the dorsal ridge of the neck, with individual hairs transitioning from lighter bases to dark tips.20 If the composition expands to include the ventral line, the distinct penis sheath must be clearly and anatomically visible.2
* Must show: High-contrast jet-black facial mask, massive thick-based horns, bulging neck muscles, prominent erect mane.
* Must avoid: Soft or slender cervical features, brown or dilute face masks, smooth horns.
* AI failure modes: The inclusion of the "black face mask" token will invariably cause the model to shift the entire body coat toward the jet-black pelage of the sable antelope. Sable horn plane errors will also occur, where the horns sweep too far back and flatten out. Negative prompt heavily against "black body coat, sable antelope, flat horns, slender neck".
C6: eating
This represents the most technically perilous prompt in the entire series due to the extreme complexity of generating a biologically accurate ruminant mouth. The shot should frame a low-angle, tight profile of the roan pulling at a clump of tall, dry savanna grass. The lighting should illuminate the interior mechanics of the mouth as the lips part to grip the tough vegetation.
The absolute, non-negotiable morphological law for this image is the complete absence of upper incisors.24 The upper jaw must feature a hard, smooth, keratinous dental pad instead of front teeth.24 The lower jaw must display a row of lower incisors, which are actively pressing the grass against the upper dental pad to sever it.24 If the lateral aspect of the mouth is visible, the render must show a wide, toothless gap known as the diastema, separating the front incisors from the rear grinding premolars and molars.26
* Must show: Upper dental pad, lower incisors, tall savanna grass being torn, wide diastema gap.
* Must avoid: Any upper front teeth, fangs, canine teeth, or flat horse-like incisors on the top jaw.
* AI failure modes: Diffusion models fail catastrophically on animal dentition, defaulting universally to human teeth, canine fangs, or equine upper incisors. You must flood the negative prompt with "human teeth, upper incisors, top teeth, horse teeth, fangs, carnivore jaw, uneven dentition, human smile".
C7: drinking
Capturing the roan drinking requires highly specific biomechanical prompting. Because the roan possesses exceptionally long legs combined with a thick, relatively short neck, it cannot simply dip its head to the water's surface like domestic cattle.16 The roan must physically alter its skeletal stance to drink. The image should place the animal at the edge of a natural, muddy African waterhole.
To lower the center of mass, the forelegs must be splayed outward laterally, spreading wide apart.27 A less common but acceptable alternative is to show one foreleg bent sharply at the carpal joint. The muzzle should just graze the water, creating subtle, physically accurate concentric ripples. The reflection cast upon the water must be anatomically consistent with the subject above it.
* Must show: Splayed or widely spread front legs, lowered neck, muzzle touching water, accurate water reflection.
* Must avoid: Standing perfectly straight while drinking, kneeling symmetrically on both carpal joints like a warthog.
* AI failure modes: AI spatial logic struggles with complex intersecting limb geometry. Splayed legs will frequently clip through each other, bend at impossible backward angles, or resolve without hooves. Water reflections frequently hallucinate entirely different species. Negative prompt against "kneeling, bent knees, clipping limbs, distorted reflection, broken water, floating legs".
C8: hooves
A dedicated macro shot of the lower limbs resting securely on dry, red, dusty African soil. The lighting should be low and raking, casting long shadows that highlight the rough, dense texture of the keratin.
The anatomy must strictly reflect an even-toed ungulate belonging to the order Artiodactyla.28 The foot must terminate in a true cloven hoof, displaying exactly two main symmetrical digits (claws) bearing the animal's weight.29 Located slightly higher up on the palmar and plantar aspects of the pastern, exactly two smaller, non-weight-bearing dewclaws must be clearly visible.28 The lower leg must be covered in the dark brown to black hair that forms the characteristic "sock" of the roan.20
* Must show: Cloven hoof split into two main toes, two distinct dewclaws, dark leg hair, dusty soil substrate.
* Must avoid: Solid horse hooves, three toes, missing dewclaws, human-like toes.
* AI failure modes: Models will routinely attempt to blend the solid hoof of a horse with antelope anatomy or create multi-toed, chimera-like monstrosities. Dewclaws are almost always forgotten or hallucinated on the front of the shin. Negative prompt: "solid hoof, horse hoof, three toes, toes, missing dewclaws, merged hooves".
C9: barely visible
This composition tests the model's ability to handle severe environmental occlusion without suffering total structural collapse. The roan should be positioned deep within a dense, tangled thicket of Vachellia tortilis (umbrella thorn) and tall, dry winter grass.30 Only fragmented anatomical markers should be visible through the dense brush, such as the sweeping curve of a horn, the flick of a tufted ear, and the high contrast of the black and white facial mask.
The lighting must be dappled, chaotic, and broken by the canopy, throwing sharp, unpredictable shadow patterns across the partially hidden animal. The thorny brush and grass in the immediate foreground must be sharply in focus, while the animal standing behind the vegetation drops slightly out of the focal plane.
* Must show: High environmental occlusion, dappled lighting, distinct roan facial mask, thick thorny brush.
* Must avoid: Floating disembodied heads, transparent grass, full body visibility.
* AI failure modes: When faced with heavy occlusion, AI processing tends to merge the texture of the foreground grass directly into the animal's fur. Alternatively, it will amputate the hidden body parts, resulting in a severed head floating unnaturally in a bush. Negative prompt: "transparent grass, severed head, floating anatomy, merged textures, structural collapse, ghosting".
C10: coat
A macro texture shot focused entirely on the broad flank of the roan. The primary purpose is to render the tactile, gritty reality of the African bushveld. The underlying skin must support a short, relatively smooth coat.1 The colour must accurately reflect the strawberry roan palette, a highly complex, pointillist blend of greyish-red, amber, and brown guard hairs overlaying a lighter base.2
To achieve true documentary realism, the coat must not be rendered as clean or pristine. It must display a coating of fine, red African dust clinging to the hairs. For advanced prompting, engineers should request a subtle, biologically accurate tick load (small, dark engorged parasites) attached near the sparse hair of the axillary region or the lower neck. This explicitly grounds the image in biological reality and breaks the artificial, digital smoothness inherent to AI generation.
* Must show: Strawberry roan hair coloration, fine dust particles, coarse hair texture, optional subtle ticks.
* Must avoid: Plastic smoothness, metallic sheen, uniform solid red colour.
* AI failure modes: The model will inevitably default to a plastic, overly-rendered 3D aesthetic or apply a golden metallic sheen. If prompted for ticks, the model may drastically overcompensate, generating massive, horrific insectoid growths. Keep parasite prompt weights extremely low. Negative prompt: "plastic, metallic, wet, shiny, smooth, solid red, giant insects, body horror".
C11: front half from side
A compositional crop designed to show only the anterior half of the roan, from the mid-torso forward. The animal should be posed looking slightly backward over its shoulder, a posture that throws the heavy musculature of the neck into sharp relief. The lighting should rake across the backline to highlight the erect dorsal mane and catch the lighter, shaggy throat beard beneath the jaw.1
The front limbs must be rendered straight, structurally sound, and mechanically correct, terminating in properly formed cloven hooves.29 The chest must appear deep and robust. The framing must cut the animal off naturally at the edge of the optical lens, avoiding clumsy, smeared AI inpainting at the borders.
* Must show: Deep chest, thick neck, erect mane, shaggy throat beard, perfectly rendered forelimbs.
* Must avoid: Awkward cropping, three front legs, reversed forelimbs.
* AI failure modes: In three-quarter or partial stands, models frequently fail to understand joint directionality, reversing the anatomy of the forelimb by putting the carpal joint on backward. It may also generate a third foreleg to balance the cropped frame. Negative prompt: "reversed joints, backward legs, three front legs, amputated torso, smeared edges".
C12: back half from side
A compositional crop focusing exclusively on the posterior half and hindquarters. The roan should be depicted walking away, angled slightly so the broad flank and rear musculature are clearly visible. The lighting should catch the sharp anatomical angles of the pelvic girdle and the prominent stifle and hock joints.
The tail is the critical anchor for this shot. It must originate smoothly and naturally from the base of the spine, hang down reasonably straight, and end in a distinct, brush-like black tuft of hair.2 The visual transition from the lighter, almost white underbelly to the strawberry roan flank must be a seamless gradient. The hind legs must prominently display the dark socks extending down to the hooves.20
* Must show: Correct hock and stifle angulation, tufted black tail, dark hind socks, seamless belly gradient.
* Must avoid: Tails originating from the mid-back, thin rat-like tails, digitigrade canine hind legs.
* AI failure modes: The latent space often fails to understand skeletal anchoring, attaching the tail improperly to the gluteal muscles rather than the spine. It may also bend the hock backward, mimicking a bird's leg or a canine digitigrade stance. Negative prompt: "displaced tail, rat tail, canine legs, backward hock, floating limbs".
C13: half face close-up
An extreme, highly cinematic macro close-up framing only the left or right half of the roan's face. The depth of field must be razor-thin to draw the viewer's eye to specific textures. The focal point must be locked onto the eye and the preorbital gland. The black and white facial mask must provide stark, geometric contrast.2
The eyelashes should appear thick, dark, and highly detailed. The preorbital gland must be clearly visible just anterior to the eye, rendering correctly as a dark, bare, trench-like slit in the skin.18 Coarse vibrissae (tactile whiskers) should be visible protruding around the muzzle. The texture of the wet nose (rhinarium) must be bumpy and slightly moist, contrasting sharply with the dry, dusty hair of the face.
* Must show: Razor-thin DOF, horizontal pupil, dark preorbital gland slit, tactile whiskers, textured rhinarium.
* Must avoid: Multiple eyes, weeping glands, blurred focus on the pupil.
* AI failure modes: As detailed in the dimorphism section, the preorbital gland will often be hallucinated as an open, bloody wound or a literal second eye. Furthermore, the horizontal pupil may collapse into a round human pupil if the macro crop is too tight for the model to register the animal context. Negative prompt: "second eye, bloody wound, human pupil, blurred eye, smooth nose".
5) Colour, light, and texture
The visual signature of reference-grade African wildlife photography relies heavily on the manipulation of lighting and texture to convey environmental authenticity. The South African bushveld presents a uniquely harsh, unforgiving light due to atmospheric scattering and particulate matter. Prompts should explicitly dictate parameters such as "golden hour light", "raking shadows", and "low angle sun" for dramatic assets, or "harsh midday bushveld sun", "blown-out sky", and "high contrast" for documentary realism.
The textural generation of the roan must aggressively combat the AI's natural tendency toward hyper-smooth, "plastic" renderings. Require prompts to include "dust-coated pelage", "coarse guard hairs", "matted sweat on the neck", and "highly detailed keratin ridges on horns". The inclusion of a subtle, biologically accurate tick load (such as Rhipicephalus species) attached to the ears or peri-anal region forces the diffusion model to render micro-textures, dramatically increasing documentary realism without forcing artificial or dynamic action. The strawberry roan coat itself must never be rendered as a solid block of colour; it must be requested as a pointillist mixture of white, grey, amber, and red hairs.2
6) Behaviour snippets for believable static poses
To avoid the artificiality of "action-movie" generations, which frequently result in anatomical collapse and limb hallucination, prompt engineers should rely on subtle ethological cues to create dynamic tension in static poses.
* Herd geometry: Roans are semi-gregarious animals with a strict hierarchy. Position a dominant female (the matriarch) in the sharp foreground, her head raised and chewing, with 3 to 5 out-of-focus herd members facing different directions in the background.20 This accurately simulates natural vigilance.
* Flight-readiness: Render the animal frozen mid-stride. Prompt for one foreleg raised slightly, weight shifted backward, ears swiveled sharply forward, and horizontally aligned pupils locked directly on the lens. This creates intense narrative tension without forcing the AI to render complex galloping biomechanics (where they can reach speeds of 57 km/h).2
* Territorial display: Depict a massive bull standing in a rigid, broadside lateral display. The thick neck should be arched downward and the horns presented forward, emphasizing maximum physical surface area to intimidate unseen rivals.15
7) Background discipline (anonymity-first)
The absolute highest priority for these assets is that they appear on a South African hunting reserve website without identifying any specific, readable geographic location. In generative engineering, identity-critical anatomy always beats pretty lighting, and anonymity always beats specific geography.
The background must be prompted as a generic, mixed woodland and grass ecotone. Engineers must use specific botanical prompts to generate thick, out-of-focus bush true to the savanna biome: Combretum apiculatum (red bushwillow, featuring distinct reddish winged seeds), Vachellia tortilis (umbrella thorn, featuring bipinnate leaves and twisted pods), and Colophospermum mopane (featuring distinctive butterfly-shaped leaves and deeply fissured bark).30
To ensure complete anonymity, apply extreme shallow depth of field parameters (f/2.8, f/1.4) so the flora melts into a heavy, unreadable bokeh mass of greens, browns, and golds. Engineers must strictly ban any anthropogenic elements using the negative prompt.
* Negative prompt framework: "readable fences, high fences, water troughs, vehicles, Land Cruisers, signs, tire tracks, kraal layouts, roads, buildings, named landmarks, powerlines".

10) CHEAT SHEET
1. Binomial: Hippotragus equinus (African bovid, Hippotraginae).
2. Coat: Strawberry roan (grey/amber/red), stark white belly.
3. Face: Black/brown mask, large white eye/muzzle patches.
4. Ears: Extremely long, narrow, dark tufts at tips.
5. Horns (Both Sexes): Scimitar arc, curved backward, heavily ridged, thick base.
6. Mane: Short, erect, stiff, dark-tipped, neck to withers.
7. Legs: Exactly four, cloven hooves, two dewclaws, dark brown/black socks.
8. Tail: Medium length, tufted black brush at end.
9. Dimorphism: Males larger (300 kg), thicker horns, darker mask, visible penis sheath.
10. Preorbital Gland: Dark bare skin slit in front of eye (NOT a second pupil).
11. Eyes: Lateral placement, horizontal pupil, deep brown iris.
12. Mouth: Ruminant. Dental pad on top, lower incisors, wide diastema (NO upper teeth).
13. Drinking Stance: Splayed/spread forelegs to reach water.
14. Habitat: Lightly wooded savanna, tall grass, water-dependent.
15. Background Flora: Combretum, Vachellia, Colophospermum mopane (defocused bokeh).
16. Negative Prompt Core: sable, black coat, kudu corkscrew, gemsbok spear, human teeth, horse hooves, round pupils, blue eyes, three legs, fences, vehicles.
