**Reference research:** `AI Image Generation Field Notes_ Buffalo & Warthog.txt` (Species B: *Phacochoerus africanus*, confusion vs domestic pig, Eurasian wild boar, bushpig, peccary, juvenile buffalo; kneeling carpal grazing and drinking; round pupil; tusks and warts; tail flagged when alarmed; anonymity-first bushveld).

**Site filenames:** **14** exports under `public/images/species/warthog/` (stems from `imageSeed: "warthog"` in `monoSeeds` plus `warthog-horns.jpg`). **Shot order is permuted** versus Cape buffalo on this sheet: **coat macro** uses the rut-left stem, **barely visible** the ecology stem, **hooves** the panorama stem (see table).

**Species target:** **Common warthog** *Phacochoerus africanus* (South African savanna, short grass pans, thicket margins). **Always** anchor prompts with **full binomial** *Phacochoerus africanus* plus **common warthog**, **African Suidae**, or **savanna grazer** context. Bare **"warthog"** alone can drift to **cartoon pig**, **feral hog**, or **farm pen** geometry. **Not** *Phacochoerus aethiopicus* (desert warthog, Horn of Africa).

**Colour morphs:** Estate colour phases **do not** apply to common warthog in realistic documentation. **Soil-toned** grey-brown skin, red dust or grey mud film. **No** fantasy morph prompts for this brief.

## READ FIRST - realism and background (every prompt)

**Realism first.** Wildlife documentary still: **lean barrel torso**, **long slender legs**, **sparse bristles** with **grey-brown skin** showing through (**no** dense wild-boar blanket), **matte dust-caked hide** (**no** plastic CGI sheen).

**Condition:** **Healthy ordinary** savanna animals. **Not** obese domestic sow silhouette, not **fat-roll** caricature.

**Background (estate-safe, anonymity-first):** Warthogs belong in **open savanna**, **short grass pans**, **thicket margins**, and **borrowed burrow** edges. **Thick foreground grass** and **low scrub** as **anonymous bokeh**: **Hyparrhenia-type gold grass**, **Vachellia** thorn scrub, **Combretum** twigs, **earthy tan and olive OOF** mass (**f/1.8–f/2.8**, long lens). Optional **mound with burrow mouth** as soft blur only (**no** readable fence line). Subject **tack-sharp** unless barely-visible slot. **No** readable **fences**, **troughs**, **vehicles**, **lodges**, **concrete floors**, **metal rails**, **tire tracks**, **ear tags**, **tracking collars** (default: **none**).

**Species lock:** *Phacochoerus africanus*. **Head:** **shovel-shaped**, disproportionately large. **Tusks:** **upper pair** curves **outward and up** in a **wide semicircle** on mature boar; **lower pair** **sharp**, **honed** against uppers at gape (**never** one fused elephant tusk, **never** downward peccary interlock). **Warts:** **three pairs** of **cartilaginous pads** (suborbital, preorbital, submaxillary), **clean callused skin**, **not** bleeding tumours.

**Coat:** **Extremely sparse** guard hairs; **prominent dorsal mane** from head crest down spine.

**Eyes:** **High and wide** on skull, **above** large suborbital warts; **round** pupil (**not** horizontal bovid oval); **medium brown iris**; coarse lashes; subtle **moist corneal highlight**.

**Ears:** **Erect**, sparsely haired (**not** heavy boar fur muffs).

**Snout:** **Broad flat rhinarium**, **dark tough leather**, **coarse tactile bristles** (**not** soft pink cartoon pig disk).

**Posture (feeding and drinking):** **Kneeling on front carpal joints** with **thick hairless calloused pads** (**not** standing buffalo-style graze at short grass). **Drinking** at water: **carpal kneel** at edge, **short neck** reaching surface.

**Tail:** **Long thin straight** shaft, **tufted tip**; when **alarmed or running**, **held rigidly upright** like a flag (**not** curly domestic corkscrew, **not** bushpig brush dragging).

**Hooves:** **Sharp dark cloven** suid hooves, **two posterior dewclaws** visible when angle allows.

**Confusion negatives:** **Domestic pig** curly tail and jowl fat; **wild boar** dense fur wedge head; **bushpig** shag red coat and long tufted ears; **peccary** collar and downward tusks; **juvenile buffalo** horn buds and bovine muzzle.

**No text, no watermark, no logos.**
