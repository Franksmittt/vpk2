## READ FIRST - realism and background (every prompt)

**Realism first.** Wildlife documentary still: **world’s largest antelope mass**, **ox-like depth**, **pronounced shoulder muscular hump**, **level back** (not gnu plunge), **small pointed bovine ears** (**not** kudu radar ears), **no** slender gazelle legs, **no** plastic CGI hide.

**Condition:** **Healthy ordinary** bulls and cows on real veld. **Not** starved, not micro-horned cows (**both sexes carry horns**).

**Background (estate-safe, anonymity-first):** Eland use **woodland glades**, **tall Hyparrhenia-type grass**, **mopane edges**, **sandy pans** nearby. **Thick bush** and grass as **foreground OOF** mass: **Burkea africana**, **Combretum**, **Vachellia**, **Colophospermum mopane**, crushed to **anonymous gold, olive, and grey-brown bokeh** (**f/1.8–f/2.8**, long lens). Animal **tack-sharp** unless barely visible slot. **No** readable **fences**, **troughs**, **vehicles**, **lodges**, **tire tracks**, **powerlines**, **kraal shapes**.

**Species lock:** *Taurotragus oryx* (Livingstone phenotype). **Coat:** **fawn to blue-grey**, often dust film; **6–10 narrow white vertical torso stripes** **distinct** vs **Cape eland** where stripes often **fade** (**tie-breaker** for your prompts). **Legs:** **dark brown band on rear of foreleg above knee**; **whitish rear pasterns** (Livingstone cue). **Bulls:** **massive pendulous dewlap** with **black tuft**, **chocolate forehead hair mat**, **thicker shorter horns** (up to ~65 cm class in literature, do not invent metre-long toys). **Cows:** **horns longer thinner** than bulls, splay slightly at tips (~68 cm class); **smaller dewlap**, **no forehead mat**, **warmer tan** coat.

**Horns (critical):** **Both sexes horned.** **Straight overall trajectory** from skull, **two tight twists** near **very thick bases**; **deep ridges**; **blunted worn tips** on old bulls. **Negative:** **kudu** wide **open** corkscrew, **wildebeest** sideways hooks, **short domestic cow** horns, **polled hornless cow**, **gemsbok spear**.

**Tail:** **Long**, **bovine**, **black tufted brush** at tip (**not** short kudu puff only, frame-dependent).

**Eyes:** **Lateral**; **horizontal rectangular** pupil; **very dark brown iris**; minimal scleral show.

**Mouth:** **Dental pad upper**, **lower incisors**, **diastema**; **never** upper front teeth.

**Drinking:** **Forelegs widely splayed** (mass + short neck physics); **dewlap** hangs vertical; **not** giraffe neck stretch with straight pillar legs.

**Confusion negatives:** **Brahman cattle** (drooping ears, exaggerated hump), **Cape eland** (faint stripes), **greater kudu** (huge ears, male-only horns, open spiral), **roan**, **wildebeest**.

**No text, no watermark, no logos.**
