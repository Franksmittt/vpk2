## READ FIRST - realism and background (every prompt)

**Realism first.** Wildlife documentary still: **massive hippotragine** (among the largest African antelope), **deep chest**, **sloping backline** (withers visibly **higher** than rump, **not** a level horse tube), **short stiff erect dorsal mane** from neck onto withers, **no** plastic CGI hide.

**Condition:** **Healthy ordinary** adults and calves on real veld. **Not** emaciated rib cages, not fantasy horns that **pass through** neck or spine.

**Background (estate-safe, anonymity-first):** Roan belong in **open woodland**, **tall grass**, and **woodland–grass ecotone**, often **water-dependent** use patterns. **Prioritize thick vegetation** as **foreground or side mass** when it helps **privacy** and **truth**: **Combretum** bushwillow, **Vachellia** umbrella thorn and thorn scrub, **Colophospermum mopane** where biome-appropriate, or **tall dry savanna grass**, all **crushed** to **anonymous olive, grey-green, tan, and gold bokeh** by **extreme shallow DOF** (about **f/1.4–f/2.8**, **200–600mm** look). Animal **tack-sharp** unless the shot type calls for slight midground softness (barely visible). **No** reconstructible farm: **no** readable **fence wire**, **gates**, **troughs**, **vehicles**, **signs**, **tire tracks**, **kraal layout**, **lodges**, **powerlines**, **roads**. **Pretty light never overrides wrong mask, horns, socks, or teeth.**

**Species lock:** *Hippotragus equinus*. **Coat:** **strawberry roan**, a **grizzled mix** of greyish-red, amber, and brown guard hairs over lighter base (**not** solid red block, **not** jet-black sable bull pelage). **Belly** **stark white** with **clean** transition to flanks (**not** muddy airbrush). **Face:** **bold black-and-white geometric mask** (intersecting bands and patches, **not** sable’s median blaze-only read, **not** gemsbok spear mask). **Females** often **softer charcoal or brownish** mask than **jet-black** dominant bulls.

**Ears (critical):** **Long**, **narrow**, almost **donkey-like** proportions; **dark brown tufts** at tips (**major** roan vs sable cue: sable ears are shorter with brick-red backs).

**Horns (critical):** **Both sexes horned.** Originate **close on frontal bone**, sweep **backward** in one **smooth scimitar arc** in a **single plane** (**no** kudu corkscrew, **no** gemsbok straight V spear, **no** wildebeest parenthetical hooks). **Lower two-thirds to three-quarters** **heavily ridged / annulated**; **distal quarter** **smooth**, **sharp**, **dark matte keratin** (**not** ivory toy, **not** chrome). **Bull:** **thicker bases**, longer arc within **believable** field range; **cow:** **slenderer**, slightly shorter, **still fully horned** (**never** hornless adult).

**Legs:** **Exactly four**; **dark brown to black socks** from about **carpal / tarsal** downward on typical phenotype (**not** gemsbok black **frontal** leg spear stripes). **Cloven** hooves; **two dewclaws** on each foot where visible.

**Mane:** **Short**, **erect**, **mohawk-like** stiff crest, dark-tipped hairs (**not** wildebeest side-falling beard sheet as the primary read).

**Tail:** **Medium length**, ends in **distinct black brush tuft** (**not** long horse tail sheet).

**Eyes:** **Lateral**; **horizontal pupil**; **deep uniform brown iris**; thick dark lashes. **Preorbital gland:** **subtle dark bare skin slit** anterior to orbit (**not** second eye, **not** weeping wound, **not** gore).

**Eyes (reject AI glamour defaults):** **No blue iris**, **no** round human pupils, **no** forward predator eye layout, **no** full human scleral ring, **no** heterochromia.

**Mouth:** **No upper incisors**; **hard upper dental pad**; **lower incisors**; **wide diastema**. **Never** upper front teeth.

**Drinking:** **Forelegs splayed wide** **or** one foreleg sharply bent to lower mass (**not** tall cattle neck-dip only, **not** symmetric warthog double carpal kneel as default).

**Confusion negatives:** **Sable** (*H. niger*) jet-black bulls, shorter ears, different mask; **gemsbok** spear horns and flank bands; **kudu** corkscrew; **blesbok / bontebok** blaze and build; **eland** mass and dewlap; **blue wildebeest** beard and gnu horns; lone **"roan"** as coat colour word without **Hippotragus equinus**.

**No text, no watermark, no logos.**
