## READ FIRST - realism and background (every prompt)

**Realism first.** Wildlife documentary still: natural ISO grain, believable muscle and hide, **no** plastic fur, **no** oversharpen, **no** HDR glow, **no** duplicated eyes or limbs, **no** cervid “deer” proportions.

**Condition:** **Healthy ordinary** wild impala. **Not** starved, not fly-blown. **Not** fantasy record horns, not airbrushed show animals.

**Background (estate-safe):** **Extreme shallow depth of field** (telephoto look **f/1.8–f/2.8** equiv). Background = **anonymous** olive-grey-tan-cream bokeh. **No** readable **fences**, **troughs**, **lodges**, **vehicles**, **signs**, **distinctive kopjes**. No postcard horizon as the subject. If soil or grass appears, keep it **generic** red-sand / buff tuft feel (Waterberg cue) **without** identifying one property.

**Species lock:** *Aepyceros melampus melampus*. **Flat to very slightly sloping backline** (no gnu hump, no hartebeest wedge face). **Long fine legs**, small neat **cloven** hooves. **Three-tone coat:** rich **reddish-fawn dorsal**, **paler tan flank**, **clean white** belly / throat / chin with **crisp** boundaries (no muddy gradient). **Black:** ear **tips**, narrow **dorsal tail stripe**, **vertical black stripes on back of thighs** (from behind, with tail stripe, reads as **“M”** on rump). **Black metatarsal tufts on hind legs only** (never on forelegs).

**Sex and horns (critical):** **Only rams** have horns: **lyrate** (S-curve), **heavy transverse ridges** on lower two-thirds, smoother dark tips; natural **asymmetry** and small chips OK; typical mature curve length **about 45–92 cm** (do not invent metre-long fantasy spikes). **Ewes are hornless:** **smooth crown**, **no pedicles**, **no horn buds**, **no** tiny spikes. **Never** put ram horns on an ewe.

**Mouth (when visible):** **No upper incisors**; **hard upper dental pad**; **lower incisors**; wide **diastema**; thick mobile **lips**. **Never** upper front teeth.

**Eyes:** **Horizontal pupil**; deep warm brown iris; **crisp white hair patch above each eye**; long **upper** lashes; calm pose **no** big human-like scleral show.

**Confusion negatives:** springbok white face and side stripe, blesbok white face blaze, wildebeest square muzzle and sloping back, **nyala vertical body stripes**, kudu calf stripes and huge ears, generic brown deer, branching antlers.

**No text, no watermark, no logos.**
