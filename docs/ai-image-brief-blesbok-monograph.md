## READ FIRST - realism and background (every prompt)

**Realism first.** Wildlife documentary still: **dense matte** coat believable for **cold Highveld nights**, **no** mirror gloss **bontebok** plum, **no** plastic fur, **no** duplicated limbs.

**Condition:** **Healthy ordinary** wild-type **dull reddish-brown** blesbok. **Yellow** or **white** estate variants optional only if explicitly requested; same anatomy and blaze rules.

**Background (estate-safe, anonymity-first):** **Highveld grassland** remains the truth (short **green** bite after burn, **dry gold** winter turf, **gentle** horizon). **Encourage thick bush** as **out-of-focus mass**: **Combretum** bushwillow, **Vachellia** thorn scrub, or **dense verge growth** in **foreground or margin**, **crushed** to **anonymous olive-brown-cream bokeh** by **extreme shallow DOF** (about **f/1.8–f/2.8**, long lens). Subject **tack-sharp**. That blur **hides** fence lines and property layout so viewers **cannot** reconstruct a specific farm from the website. **No** readable **wire**, **gates**, **troughs**, **lodges**, **vehicles**, **signs**, **tire tracks**, **kraal geometry**, **named kopjes**. **No** rocky mountain spine as default, **no** Kalahari dune story unless sourced.

**Species lock:** *Damaliscus pygargus phillipsi*. **Sloping backline:** **shoulders visibly higher** than rump (**not** level deer tube, **not** horse rectangle). **Coat:** **dull earthy reddish-brown**; **lighter yellowish-brown saddle** on mid-back; **pale tan to yellowish-white rump** (**distinctly not** **pure optic-white** bontebok sheet). **Tail** short, **bushy black tuft** (**not** long fluffy white deer tail).

**Face (critical):** **White facial blaze** that is **broken** by a **horizontal band of brown fur across the face above the eyes** (**must** read as **interrupted** blaze, **not** one continuous white mask from horns to nose). Dark fur **separates** blaze margins from **eyes** (**no** white fur **invading** the socket).

**Horns (critical):** **Both sexes horned.** **S-shaped** profile: rise from skull, sweep **back** and slightly **out**, tips hook **slightly forward**; **heavy rings / ridges** over ~**85–90%** of length, **smooth sharp** tips. **Straw-coloured, yellowish-brown, or pale wear** on **upper ridge crests** is typical (**not** uniformly **jet-black** polished bontebok look). Ewe horns **slenderer** and slightly shorter than bull; **never** hornless adult.

**Eyes:** **Horizontal oval or slit pupil**; warm brown iris; **prominent dark preorbital scent gland** as **small moist depression** **below and slightly forward** of eye (**show** in portraits where angle allows). **No** white blaze hair **touching** the globe.

**Eyes (reject AI glamour defaults):** **No blue iris**, **no icy or sapphire eyes**, **no** full **human sclera** display, **no heterochromia**, **no** LED glow. Both eyes: **natural brown** and **horizontal** pupil logic.

**Anatomy integrity (especially 2+ legs visible):** **Exactly four legs**; **bilateral** forelimb and hindlimb consistency (**no** one forelimb reversed); **cloven** dark hooves; **white stockings** on lower legs with **outer and front** surfaces often **remaining dark brown** (**not** **all-white** legs like a bontebok advert variant unless intentionally that morph).

**Mouth:** **No upper incisors**; **hard upper dental pad**; **lower incisors**; **broad flat grazing muzzle**. **Never** upper front teeth.

**Confusion negatives:** **Bontebok** (unbroken blaze, purple-black gloss, **bright white** rump), **springbok** (side stripe, lyre inward-hook horns, white face streak not blaze band), **hartebeest**, generic **deer**.

**No text, no watermark, no logos.**
