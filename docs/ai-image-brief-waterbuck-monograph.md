## READ FIRST - realism and background (every prompt)

**Realism first.** Wildlife documentary still: natural grain, believable mass, **no** plastic fur, **no** oversharpen, **no** HDR glow, **no** duplicated limbs, **no** impala- or deer-like long thin legs on this animal.

**Condition:** **Healthy ordinary** wild waterbuck. **Not** starved, not a cartoon bull. **Not** silky show-ring grooming.

**Background (estate-safe):** **Extreme shallow DOF** for portraits (about **f/2–f/4** telephoto look). Background = **anonymous** green-gold grass bokeh, soft mud bank tones, thorn scrub blobs. **No** readable **fences**, **troughs**, **lodges**, **vehicles**, **signs**, or **named kopjes**.

**Species lock:** *Kobus ellipsiprymnus ellipsiprymnus*. **Heavy barrel chest**, **long torso**, **short powerful legs**, **very thick shaggy neck** (optical lift at withers OK from mane, **not** gnu-grade spinal plunge). **Coarse grizzled grey-brown** coat, often **slightly oily / matted** sheen (semi-aquatic secretions), **not** mirror gloss.

**Rump (critical):** **Crisp hollow white ellipse** around tail insertion on dark haunch (**ring**, not solid white rump sheet).

**Legs (critical):** **Solid black lower legs** with a **sharp white hair band** immediately **above** the **broad black cloven** hooves (separates from nyala orange socks, wildebeest without ankle ring).

**Horns (critical):** **Males only.** **Never** horns, pedicles, or horn buds on females. Rams: **widely spaced bases**, then three **broad** segments in order: (1) sweep **backward** along or above the neck line, (2) flare **outward** to the sides, (3) tips hook **forward**. Whole horn reads as an **open** wide **lyre** or shallow **S** in mostly **one plane** beside the head (**flat-ish** curve, not a tube coiled in space). **Heavy annulations** on basal two-thirds to three-quarters, **smooth** tips; length about **55–99 cm** along curve; slight asymmetry and chips OK. **Never** a **closed circle**, **never** a **tight spiral** or **corkscrew**, **never** **sheep-** or **mouflon-style** full curls, **never** kudu twists.

**Female:** **Strictly hornless**, smooth crown; **slighter** frame than bull; coat often **slightly lighter** grizzle; same face pattern (white muzzle, white eyebrow stripes, dark rhinarium).

**Eyes:** **Horizontal slit pupil**; dark iris; **bold white eyebrow** above eye; lateral placement. Calm: minimal scleral show.

**Mouth:** **No upper incisors**; **hard upper dental pad**; **lower incisors**; tongue wraps grass. **Never** upper front teeth.

**Confusion negatives:** nyala (vertical flank stripes, spiral horns male), lechwe / puku (different rump, wetland build), roan (shorter curved horns, standing mane), generic deer proportions, **defassa solid rump**.

**No text, no watermark, no logos.**
