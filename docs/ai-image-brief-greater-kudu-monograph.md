## READ FIRST - realism and background (every prompt)

**Realism first.** Wildlife documentary still: **tall athletic browser**, **high withers**, **long legs**, **large rounded ears** with **pale pinkish inner pinna**, **no** ox-like eland bulk, **no** plastic CGI hide.

**Condition:** **Healthy ordinary** wild-type bulls and hornless cows. **Not** emaciated, not fantasy corkscrews that **self-intersect** or pass through the neck.

**Background (estate-safe, anonymity-first):** Kudu **belong** in **dense thicket**, **broken woodland**, **mopane** edges, and **rocky red-soil** slopes. **Prioritize thick vegetation** as **foreground or side mass**: **Combretum apiculatum**, **Vachellia robusta** or thorn scrub, **Colophospermum mopane**, **tall golden winter grass**, all **crushed** to **anonymous olive, grey-green, tan, and rust bokeh** by **extreme shallow DOF** (about **f/1.8–f/2.8**, **200–600mm** look). Subject **tack-sharp** unless the slot is barely visible. **No** readable **fence wire**, **troughs**, **vehicles**, **lodges**, **tire tracks**, **kraal geometry**, **signs**, **powerlines**. **Pretty light never overrides stripe count, horn sex rules, or teeth.**

**Species lock:** *Tragelaphus strepsiceros*. **Coat:** **tan-grey to blue-grey** (mature bulls **dustier blue-grey**); **6–10 thin crisp white vertical flank stripes** (**not** 11–15 lesser-kudu density, **not** nyala spots / broken chaos). **Belly** relatively **clean** (**no** nyala **shaggy ventral fringe**). **Males:** **long ventral throat mane** and **dorsal crest**; **cows:** **hornless**, **clean slender neck**, **tawny to reddish-grey** coat. **Forelegs:** **dark garters** (bands) **above the carpal joint**.

**Horns (critical):** **Males only.** **Wide-spread corkscrew**, about **2.5–3** full **open** twists in **one plane**, **slanting back** from frontal insert; **heavy annulations** on lower shaft, **smoother worn tips**. **Females:** **no horns, no pedicle stubs** in adults. **Negative:** eland **tight** spiral, **branched deer antlers**, **gemsbok spear**, **nyala** 1.5–2 twist **ivory-tipped** lyrate look.

**Ears:** **Disproportionately large**, **wide**, **rounded** (major tie-breaker vs eland’s **small pointed** ears).

**Tail:** **Bushy**, **dark tip**, **white underside** visible when raised on alarm.

**Eyes:** **Lateral**; **horizontal** pupil (slit to rectangle); **brown iris**; thick **pale lashes** angling down. **Preorbital gland:** **small dark moist slit** forward of orbit (**not** wound, **not** second pupil).

**Mouth:** **No upper incisors**; **hard dental pad**; **lower incisors**; **diastema**. **Never** upper front teeth.

**Drinking:** **Splayed forelegs** or **deep shoulder flex** to reach water (**not** default carpal kneel like warthog).

**Confusion negatives:** **Nyala** (orange socks, shaggy belly, male stripes messy, **horns on ewes** in nyala males only for horns on males), **eland** (dewlap, hump, faint stripes, **both sexes horned**), **lesser kudu** (more stripes, white neck chevron not throat mane), **bongo**, **impala**, **generic deer**.

**No text, no watermark, no logos.**
