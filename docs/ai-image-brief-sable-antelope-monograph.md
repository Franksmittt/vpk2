## READ FIRST - realism and background (every prompt)

**Realism first.** Wildlife documentary still: **heavy Hippotragus build** (deep chest, **stiff upright dorsal mane**, level strong back), **no** slender kudu frame, **no** wildebeest plunging backline, **no** plastic CGI hide.

**Condition:** **Healthy ordinary** estate or wild-type *H. n. niger*. **Not** emaciated, not fantasy horn arcs that **intersect the spine** or pass through the neck.

**Background (estate-safe, anonymity-first):** Sable **belong** in **mixed woodland / bushveld** and **grass–woodland ecotone**. **Prioritize thick vegetation** when it helps **privacy** and **truth**: **Combretum** bushwillow, **Vachellia** thorn scrub, **Colophospermum mopane** where biome-appropriate, or **tall Themeda** grass as **foreground or side mass**, all **crushed** to **anonymous olive, grey-green, and tan bokeh** by **extreme shallow DOF** (about **f/1.8–f/2.8**, **200–600mm** look). Animal **tack-sharp**. The goal is **real veld** plus **no** reconstructible farm: **no** readable **fence wire**, **gates**, **troughs**, **plastic feed bins**, **lodges**, **vehicles**, **signs**, **tire tracks**, **cattle kraal layout**, **distinctive building silhouettes**. Open grass patches are fine **if** background stays **unidentifiable** blur. **Pretty light never overrides wrong horns or teeth.**

**Species lock:** *Hippotragus niger niger*. **Mature bull:** **glossy jet-black** body (not grey roan, not nyala charcoal with flank stripes). **Female / young:** **rich chestnut to dark brown** (not hornless template). **Shared:** **brilliant white** belly and **white rump patch** with **sharp** boundary to dark flanks (**no** muddy gradient). **Face:** **white cheeks** with **wide black median blaze** on nose bridge and **black stripes** from **eye toward muzzle** (**not** roan **clown mask** patchwork, not gemsbok spear mask).

**Horns (critical):** **Both sexes horned.** Insert on **frontal bone** just **above / slightly behind** orbits. Rise **near-vertical**, then **one smooth backward scimitar arc** in **a single plane** (**no** lateral corkscrew, **no** kudu spiral, **no** gemsbok straight spear). **Lower two-thirds to three-quarters** **heavily annulated**; **distal section** **smooth**, **sharp**, **matte black or dark grey keratin** (**not** chrome metal). **Bull:** massive **basal girth**, long arc within **believable published range** (do not invent lengths that **wrap past the shoulder** or **fuse** into the back). **Ewe:** **slenderer**, **shorter**, **shallower** curve (**never** hornless adult).

**Ears:** Relatively **short**, **brick red** on back (not nyala huge rounded ears).

**Mane:** **Stiff black dorsal crest** from poll toward withers (**not** a long horse mane falling to the side).

**Tail:** **Moderately long**, **relatively slim**, **dark terminal tuft** (**not** horse tail cascade).

**Eyes:** **Lateral**; **horizontal pupil** (wider when dilated); **deep brown iris**; subtle **preorbital gland** forward of orbit (**subtle**, not bleeding wound).

**Eyes (reject AI glamour defaults):** **No blue iris**, **no** sapphire / icy eyes, **no** heterochromia, **no** full human **scleral ring**, **no** round forward predator pupils, **no** tapetum **laser glow** unless scene is explicitly **night** with sourced behaviour.

**Anatomy integrity:** **Exactly four legs**; **bilateral** forelimb and hindlimb logic (**no** one reversed cannon); **cloven** hooves; **dark legs**, **no** gemsbok-style **white socks** above hooves (inner thigh white patches OK if sourced). **Drinking:** **splayed forelegs** or **carpal kneel**, **not** rubber neck from upright stance.

**Mouth:** **No upper incisors**; **hard upper dental pad**; **lower incisors**; **wide diastema**. **Never** upper front teeth.

**Confusion negatives:** **Roan** (*H. equinus*) grey-roan coat, **long ear tufts**, shallower horn arc, different facial **clown** layout; **gemsbok** spear horns, flank stripe, socks; **male nyala** lyrate yellow-tipped horns, vertical body stripes, belly fringe; **greater kudu** corkscrew; **blue wildebeest** beard and parenthetical horns, sloping gnu back; lone **"sable"** without **Hippotragus niger**.

**No text, no watermark, no logos.**
