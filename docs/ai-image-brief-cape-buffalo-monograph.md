## READ FIRST - realism and background (every prompt)

**Realism first.** Wildlife documentary still: **massive barrel chest**, **straight level backline** (**no** American bison **shoulder hump**), **deep neck** carrying horn mass, **sparse coarse dark hair**, **matte dust-caked hide** (**no** plastic CGI sheen).

**Condition:** **Healthy ordinary** herd animals or solitary **daga**-type old bulls. **Not** emaciated, not toy-clean zoo polish.

**Background (estate-safe, anonymity-first):** Buffalo belong in **savanna**, **bushveld**, **mopane**, and **riverine** edge. **Thick foreground vegetation** and **tall dry grass** as **anonymous bokeh**: **Colophospermum mopane**, **Vachellia karroo** or thorn scrub, **Combretum imberbe** (leadwood) shapes, **golden Hyparrhenia-type grass**, crushed to **olive, grey-brown, and tan OOF** mass (**f/1.8–f/2.8**, long lens). Subject **tack-sharp** unless barely-visible slot. **No** readable **fences**, **troughs**, **vehicles**, **lodges**, **tire tracks**, **kraal layout**, **powerlines**, **ear tags**, **tracking collars** unless explicitly documenting research capture (default: **none**).

**Species lock:** *Syncerus caffer caffer*. **Boss (mature bull):** **fused central keratin shield** across forehead, **heavily ridged**, **asymmetrical wear**; horns **drop along face** then **hook up**; span within **believable** field class (**no** hair strip **between** fused boss on mature bull). **Cow:** **shorter thinner horns**, **separate bases**, **hair on crown between bases** (**no** full fused boss). **Both sexes horned.**

**Ears:** **Large**, **drooping below horn curve**, **sparse fringe** hairs on margins (**not** tiny bison ears hidden in fur).

**Eyes:** **Wide on skull**; **horizontal oval** pupil; **medium brown iris**; thick dark lashes; slight **moist sheen** (**not** round human pupil, **not** vertical cat slit).

**Muzzle:** **Broad flat** grazing muzzle; **hard upper dental pad**; **lower incisors**; **no upper front teeth**.

**Tail:** **Long rope-like** (roughly **hock reach** class), **dark tufted brush** tip (**not** short bison rump stub, **not** wildebeest horse cascade as primary read).

**Hooves:** **Massive cloven** front feet (**wide** relative to rear), **two dewclaws** high on rear of pastern; **mud in sulci**, wall cracks OK.

**Drinking:** **Standing on all four**, **neck extended**, **muzzle** at water plane (**not** default carpal kneel).

**Confusion negatives:** **Bison** hump and shag; **water buffalo** smooth crescent horns; **domestic cattle** udder caricatures; **wildebeest** beard and gnu back; **eland** spiral; **rhino** nasal horn.

**No text, no watermark, no logos.**
