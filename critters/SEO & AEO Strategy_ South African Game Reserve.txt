	3. Entity and Business Truth Definition

Vaalpenskraal benefits from deep geographic and historical roots in the region, with historical texts noting the area's ties to the Crocodile and Matlabas rivers, Anglo-Boer War movements, and trondhjemitic gneisses.3 Anchoring the estate to these immutable geographic entities strengthens the overall Knowledge Graph. Furthermore, defining exact infrastructure—such as the Vark Kraal and the exact 4-chalet layout—creates a unique digital fingerprint.

* Entity Modification Regex: .*(waterberg|limpopo|matlabas|vark kraal).*
   * Purpose: Measures geographic and specific infrastructure brand awareness. Increases here demonstrate that the entity graph is successfully binding the Vaalpenskraal brand to these specific physical locations.

   * Purpose: Tracks the bottom-of-funnel queries where users are evaluating the operational liability of the estate. High impressions on these terms prove that the Trust and Compliance pillars are successfully intercepting anxious buyers.

Defining and Measuring AEO "Wins"
